## 🧭 About

- 🔭 **Data Engineering Intern** building metadata-driven **Microsoft Fabric** pipelines — medallion architecture, ServiceNow & Wrike ingestion, PySpark on lakehouses.
- 🧪 **Research focus:** causal discovery in financial & macroeconomic time series, Bayesian methods, and applied ML.
- 📊 **Background:** B.S. Statistics + M.S. Information Technology. I live where statistics, finance, health economics, and real-world data collide.
- 🎥 I run **Statistically Lethal** on YouTube — breaking down statistics & finance concepts.
- ⚡ Currently shipping: a Wrike→Fabric medallion pipeline, a causal-architecture paper, and quant research side projects.
